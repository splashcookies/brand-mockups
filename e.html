The Everything

Makes 6 · 25 minutes · One bowl

Ingredients

  • 2¼ cups all-purpose flour, measured properly
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • ¾ tsp fine sea salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened but not melted
  • 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 2 eggs, room temperature
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 6 oz dark chocolate, chopped
  • 4 oz milk chocolate, chopped
  • ½ cup toffee bits
  • ½ cup soft caramel pieces
  • 1 cup pretzels, broken
  • ¾ cup rolled oats
  • ½ cup dried cherries
  • 1 Splash kit
  • 1 scoop water — scoop’s in the box

Method

  1. Weigh the flour. Do not scoop it from the bag.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda and salt in a second bowl.
  3. Cream the butter and both sugars until pale, 4 minutes.
  4. Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla.
  5. Fold the dry into the wet. Stop before it is smooth.
  6. Chop the chocolate. Fold in every add-in.
  7. Chill the dough 24 hours. Yes, really.
  8. Heat the oven to 350°F. Parchment from the box onto a baking sheet, medium bowl out.
  9. Empty both pouches into the bowl with one full scoop of water. Gloves on, mix by hand for two to three minutes.
  10. Scoop six, a little bigger than a golf ball, onto the parchment in the box.
  11. Bake 12–15 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centres still look soft.
  12. Cool ten minutes. Tempting as it is, do not skip this one.

1

Mix

Both pouches into one bowl, one full scoop of water, gloves on. It comes together by hand in two to three minutes and it never needs a mixer, because the hard part — getting the butter and sugar right — happened before it was sealed.

Three minutes

2

Scoop

Six scoops, a little bigger than a golf ball, onto the parchment that came in the box already cut to your tray. Leave them room. They spread, and they are supposed to.

Seven minutes

3

Bake

350°F, twelve to fifteen minutes, edges set and centres still soft. They keep cooking on the tray, so pull them a minute before you think. Resting ten minutes is the official advice and nobody has ever taken it.

Fifteen minutes
