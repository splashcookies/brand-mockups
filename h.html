Minute 0

Mix

Both pouches into one bowl with one full scoop of water. Gloves on, hands in. Nothing to measure — even the scoop is pre-sized.

3 minutes

Minute 3

Scoop

Six balls on the parchment that came cut to your tray. Leave them room — they spread, and they are meant to.

7 minutes

Minute 10

Bake

350°F, twelve to fifteen minutes, out while the edges are golden and the middles still look soft.

15 minutes

Minute 25

Cool

Ten minutes on the tray, where they finish cooking. Tempting as it is, this is not the step to skip.

10 minutes

Five things in the box. The sixth is water, and that one’s yours.

Two chocolates, toffee, caramel, pretzels, oats and dried cherries are already in the dry pouch. You supply a tap.

Why it comes out the same every time

Our mom spent years taking the guesswork out.

Michele is a chemical engineer by trade and a baker by heart. She kept running this recipe until it stopped depending on who was holding the spoon.

Everything she pinned down — the flour to the gram, the butter, the timing — is the part you no longer have to get right.
