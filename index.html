The Everything

A premium recipe, pre-measured to perfection · bakes 6

You want to show up with something delicious, but you don't have the afternoon it takes. The Everything does the hard part: two kinds of chocolate, toffee, caramel, pretzels, oats, and dried cherries, every ingredient pre-measured and ready when you are.

  • Six gourmet cookies: sweet, salty, chewy, crunchy
  • 25 minutes, start to finish
  • One dry pouch (add-ins built in), one wet pouch
  • Only item from your kitchen? Water.
Get on the list →

Mix. Scoop. Bake.

  1. Step 01

    Dry pouch into a bowl, add-ins already in. Pour in the wet pouch and water. Mix.

    ~3 minutes

  2. Step 02

    Six a-little-larger-than golf-ball sized scoops on the parchment we included.

    ~7 minutes

  3. Step 03

    350°F, 12 to 15 minutes. Edges golden, centers soft. Cool for 10.

    ~15 minutes
