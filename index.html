Landing page mockups

Directions for the splashbaking.com landing page after the October 2026 rebrand. Same copy and photos throughout; nothing is wired up. Open any one and use the bar at the bottom, or the number keys, to move between them.

Round 5 — the merge

  1. 1The counterBakery counter’s light, shoppable, photo-led world with the tear-open pouch as the living half of its two-panel hero. Real Everything photography, packaging-accurate copy, hand-lettering kept as a system.→

Round 4

  1. 2The bakeScroll position is oven time. A pinned stage takes the cookie from raw dough through the oven to done as you move down, the cavity glowing up around it, clock and temperature counting alongside.→
  2. 3Michele’s notebookThe whole site as her test-kitchen book — ruled paper, taped photos, marker arrows, sticky notes. The signature is the batch log: forty-seven attempts, forty-six crossed out.→
  3. 4Tear it openThe hero is a sealed pouch you pull apart; the contents spill out and name themselves. Drags, takes arrow keys, and opens on its own if you’d rather not.→

Round 3

  1. 5Lab notebookThe kit documented like a part: typed-report monospace, an exploded parts diagram, and one deadpan chart about how much a cup of flour actually weighs.→
  2. 6Recipe cardThe page is the recipe — and the from-scratch ingredient list is struck through line by line until only “1 Splash kit” is left.→
  3. 7Kitchen partyBaking as the activity, loud. The guide draws its colours as irregular blobs, so blobs become the shape language: blob photos, blob cards, sticker badges, a mess-o-meter.→
  4. 8Shelf readyAs if it were already on the shelf at Target. Hard colour blocks, one claim at a time, shelf-talker tags, and a Cookie Facts panel.→

Round 2

  1. 9Cream & lineGraza + Fishwife. Bake video hero, line illustrations with typewriter captions, giant mark at the foot.→
  2. 0Bakery counterLevain + Deux. Shoppable and photo-led: icon ticker, tilted photos on pink and blue panels, big quote.→
  3. -The dropLast Crumb + Fly By Jing. Chocolate ground, countdown to the drop, poster-size manifesto, loud peach family section.→
