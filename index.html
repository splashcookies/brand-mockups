Landing page mockups

Directions for the splashbaking.com landing page after the October 2026 rebrand. Round 5 is the one we settled on — it is four linked pages rather than a single screen. Everything below it is kept as the record of how we got there, and is out of date on purpose. Nothing is wired up: the forms and the cart only confirm, and no card details are collected anywhere.

Round 5 — chosen

  1. 1HomeThe counter. A photograph of six cookies is the hero, with no pull control and nothing laid over it; Wonder Woman has no section of her own and crosses the page once as the kit goes by. Twenty-one colour options live behind ?theme= — the bar at the foot switches hero treatments.→
  2. 2ShopThe Everything on the shelf, with two unannounced flavors shown plainly as not for sale — no price, no link.→
  3. 3The EverythingWhere buying happens: gallery, pack sizes, a free-shipping progress bar, a cart drawer and a Stripe hand-off. It stops before any card field.→
  4. 4Our storyMichele, in the words already on splashbaking.com/pages/our-story, set in the new type and colour rather than rewritten.→
  5. 5Our peopleThe wall for tagged photos. Splash’s own photography and empty slots — no invented customers, handles or quotes.→

Round 4 — archive

  1. 6The bakeScroll position is oven time. A pinned stage takes the cookie from raw dough through the oven to done as you move down, the cavity glowing up around it, clock and temperature counting alongside.→
  2. 7Michele’s notebookThe whole site as her test-kitchen book — ruled paper, taped photos, marker arrows, sticky notes. The signature is the batch log: forty-seven attempts, forty-six crossed out.→
  3. 8Tear it openThe hero is a sealed pouch you pull apart; the contents spill out and name themselves. Drags, takes arrow keys, and opens on its own if you’d rather not.→

Round 3 — archive

  1. 9Lab notebookThe kit documented like a part: typed-report monospace, an exploded parts diagram, and one deadpan chart about how much a cup of flour actually weighs.→
  2. 10Recipe cardThe page is the recipe — and the from-scratch ingredient list is struck through line by line until only “1 Splash kit” is left.→
  3. 11Kitchen partyBaking as the activity, loud. The guide draws its colors as irregular blobs, so blobs become the shape language: blob photos, blob cards, sticker badges, a mess-o-meter.→
  4. 12Shelf readyAs if it were already on the shelf at Target. Hard color blocks, one claim at a time, shelf-talker tags, and a Cookie Facts panel.→

Round 2 — archive

  1. 13Cream & lineGraza + Fishwife. Bake video hero, line illustrations with typewriter captions, giant mark at the foot.→
  2. 14Bakery counterLevain + Deux. Shoppable and photo-led: icon ticker, tilted photos on pink and blue panels, big quote.→
  3. 15The dropLast Crumb + Fly By Jing. Chocolate ground, countdown to the drop, poster-size manifesto, loud peach family section.→
