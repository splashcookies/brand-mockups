Batch 47 is the one in the box. The overnight chill from 38 happens before it’s sealed, so you don’t have to.

Three things broke it every time.

So those three are the ones that got taken off your side of the counter.

FlourA scooped cup is 120–150 g depending on the hand. Now it’s weighed.
ButterToo cold, no spread. Too warm, one giant cookie. Now it’s already creamed.
MixingSmooth dough means tough cookies. Now you mix by hand and stop early.

What’s left for you.

  • Both pouches in a bowl with one full scoop of water. Gloves on, mix by hand.Two to three minutes. Stop as soon as there’s no dry flour — lumpy is correct.
  • Six scoops on the parchment, well apart.Seven minutes, and the job to hand to a five-year-old.
  • 350°F, 12–15 minutes. Out while the edges are golden and the middles still look soft.They keep cooking on the tray. Trust it.
  • Cool ten minutes.While tempting, do not skip this step. It’s worth the wait.
