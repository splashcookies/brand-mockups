Our story

Born from family baking, a whole lot of tinkering, and a little bit of magic.

If you know our mom, Michele, you know she walks through the world with some extra magic, somehow turning a list of 1,000 to-do’s into one streamlined, efficient operation. As her children, we have always been impressed by her ability to do it all with grace and a smile. She balanced being the type of mom who did “Wednesday Breakfast In Bed” with working full-time, running marathons and volunteering at our schools.

Over the years, we have learned so much by observing the way she lives: she infuses efficiency with love and service.

That’s why it makes sense to us that Splash woke her up in the middle of the night as an idea that could help others do the same.

Splash makes the joy of baking gourmet easier than ever.

No mess, no measuring, no stress. Just mix, scoop, bake… and enjoy. Gourmet flavor. Zero fuss. Pure sweetness.
