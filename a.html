Cookies so fresh,you baked them.

Mix · 3 min

Dry pouch into a bowl. Pour in the wet pouch and water. Stir until it looks like dough, because it is.

Scoop · 7 min

Six scoops, a little bigger than a golf ball, on the parchment we included.

Bake · 15 min

350°F, 12 to 15 minutes. Edges golden, centers soft. Cool for ten if you can stand it.
