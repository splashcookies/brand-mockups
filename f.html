Three things to do. Two things to wait for.

Heat the oven, then mix, scoop and bake. The fifth step is letting them cool, which is genuinely the hard one.

Step one

Mix

Both pouches in a bowl, one full scoop of water — the scoop comes with it. Gloves on, hands are the mixer.

Step two

Scoop

Six balls on the parchment we already cut for you. This is the job you hand to a five-year-old.

Step three

Bake

350°F for twelve to fifteen minutes. Then the hard part: letting them cool.
